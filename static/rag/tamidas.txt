Why TamidaS CMMS?

TamidaS CMMS is a comprehensive maintenance management solution that helps organizations optimize their maintenance operations, reduce downtime, and extend asset life. With powerful features for both reactive and proactive maintenance, our platform ensures your equipment runs at peak performance while minimizing costs.

From breakdown maintenance to condition-based monitoring, asset inventory to employee attendance—TamidaS CMMS provides everything you need to manage maintenance operations efficiently in one integrated platform.



##Breakdown Maintenance

Respond quickly to equipment failures with our streamlined breakdown maintenance module. Log breakdowns instantly, assign technicians, track repair progress, and analyze failure patterns to prevent future occurrences.

Instant breakdown reporting from mobile or desktop
Real-time status tracking and notifications
Detailed failure analysis and root cause documentation


##Preventive Maintenance

Prevent equipment failures before they happen with automated preventive maintenance scheduling. Create maintenance plans, set recurring schedules, and ensure all critical assets receive timely attention to maximize uptime and extend equipment life.

Automated PM work order generation based on schedules
Customizable maintenance checklists and procedures
Parts and labor cost tracking for each PM task
Compliance documentation and audit trails


##Condition-based Maintenance

Leverage real-time equipment condition monitoring to trigger maintenance only when needed. Monitor parameters like vibration, temperature, pressure, and performance metrics to predict failures and schedule maintenance at the optimal time.

Real-time sensor data integration and monitoring
Configurable thresholds and alert triggers
Automatic work order creation when conditions are met
Trend analysis and predictive failure detection


##PM Calendar

Visualize all your preventive maintenance activities in one comprehensive calendar view. Plan ahead, avoid scheduling conflicts, balance workload across teams, and ensure no critical maintenance task is missed with our intuitive PM calendar.

Monthly, weekly, and daily calendar views
Color-coded activities by priority and status
Drag-and-drop rescheduling for easy planning
Resource allocation and workload balancing


##Assets Inventory

Maintain a complete digital inventory of all your assets with detailed specifications, maintenance history, documentation, and lifecycle tracking. From equipment registration to retirement, manage every aspect of your asset lifecycle in one centralized system.

Complete asset registry with specifications and documentation
QR code and barcode scanning for quick asset identification
Maintenance history and cost tracking per asset
Asset hierarchy and location tracking
